🟦 THE SENTENCE & TYPES

1. A group of words that makes complete sense is called:
A) Clause
B) Phrase
C) Sentence
D) Expression
Ans: C

2. Which sentence is Interrogative?
A) Be quiet.
B) What a shame!
C) Where do you live?
D) He sat on a wall.
Ans: C

3. “Have mercy upon us.” is:
A) Declarative
B) Imperative
C) Exclamatory
D) Interrogative
Ans: B

4. Which expresses strong feeling?
A) Assertive
B) Imperative
C) Interrogative
D) Exclamatory
Ans: D
